# AI assistance ledger

This ledger supports the mandatory ICLR 2027 manuscript and submission-form disclosure. It is not a substitute for the authors' final review of the disclosure categories.

## Tasks assisted

- Literature discovery and question answering over papers retrieved through alphaXiv.
- Drafting and revising experiment code, tests, artifact manifests, and reproduction instructions under human-specified goals.
- Planning the manuscript structure and drafting prose from a frozen claim ledger.
- Editing for clarity, consistency, and removal of formulaic phrasing.
- Formatting and compiling the manuscript with the official ICLR 2027 files.

## Controls and verification

- The confirmation panel, hypotheses, estimands, exclusions, multiplicity procedure, compute cap, and stopping rule were committed before confirmation outcomes.
- Controller predictions are committed before intervention outcomes are generated.
- Quantitative statements are generated from immutable result artifacts rather than supplied from model memory.
- Bibliographic metadata and scientific attributions are checked against retrieved primary sources.
- Adverse outcomes, null results, and limitations remain in the paper and artifact package.
- Authors remain responsible for the work, review all generated content, and make the final submission decisions.

## Excluded practices

- No perplexity target is used to imitate a named author's prose or conceal assistance.
- No detector-evasion objective is used.
- No unfavorable planned result or required comparison is removed.
- No author, affiliation, conflict, sanction status, or submission-form answer is inferred or altered.
- The alphaXiv reader was unavailable for the second round; added literature entries were read from the arXiv HTML and abstract endpoints, and the ledger records that substitution.
