We find that cross-draw control is the strongest policy on the event-triggered panel, at 56.24\% success against 54.97\% for continuation and 54.30\% for the best fixed repair. The denominator contains all 593 planned tasks: 497 reached a triggered checkpoint and 96 produced no trigger before termination. Against continuation we estimate 1.26 percentage points (floorplan-bootstrap 95\% interval [$-$0.35, 2.83]; group sign-flip $p=0.1579$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.1579$), which the frozen test does not certify. Against the best fixed repair we estimate 1.94 points ([$-$0.17, 4.17]; $p=0.0855\text{ (unadjusted)}$); we pre-specified that contrast as secondary and did not size the panel to resolve it.

The rule we deploy selects continuation on 298 of 497 triggered prefixes, the warning on 36, and the relocation message on 163; it leaves the remaining arms unused. It fires on 40.0\% of triggered prefixes and records 52 draw-level recoveries against 37 disruptions, versus 105 and 113 for the unconditional repair.
