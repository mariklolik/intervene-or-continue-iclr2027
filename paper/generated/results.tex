The ALFWorld denominator contains all 384 planned tasks: 355 reached the frozen checkpoint and 29 ended earlier. Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-policies} reports the full policy census. Direct signed-benefit prediction achieves 54.43\% success and the matched controller achieves 54.82\%. Relative to continuation, the direct effect is 1.56 percentage points (group-bootstrap 95\% interval [$-$0.12, 3.41]; group sign-flip $p=0.1048$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.2096$). Relative to the matched controller it is $-$0.39 points ([$-$2.26, 1.57]; adjusted $p=0.7927$). Neither primary controller comparison reaches significance at the unadjusted level, so neither is eliminated by the multiplicity correction alone. The development-selected safe policy changes success by 1.95 points relative to continuation. These tests answer the frozen comparisons; a favorable unadjusted subset is not substituted for them.

Direct selects continuation on 245/355 eligible prefixes, warning on 4, replanning on 106, and rollback on 0; it records 26 draw-level recoveries and 14 disruptions. Matched intervenes on 78.0\% of prefixes, with 66 recoveries and 51 disruptions. The unconditional repair reaches 55.34\% with 74 recoveries against 55 disruptions: Direct is the more precise rule and the unconditional rule is the more productive one, because firing on 31.0\% of prefixes forgoes the net recoveries that firing everywhere collects. Direct uses 27.4 calls, 42560 input tokens, and 63.4 client-seconds per planned task, versus 28.1, 43557, and 64.9 for continuation. These summaries are descriptive.
