The ALFWorld denominator contains all 384 planned tasks: 355 reached the frozen checkpoint and 29 ended earlier. Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-policies} reports the full policy census. Direct signed-benefit prediction achieves 54.43\% success and the matched controller achieves 54.82\%. Relative to continuation, the direct effect is 1.56 percentage points (group-bootstrap 95\% interval [\ensuremath{-0.12}, 3.41]; group sign-flip $p=0.1048$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.2096$). Relative to the matched controller it is \ensuremath{-0.39} percentage points (group-bootstrap 95\% interval [\ensuremath{-2.26}, 1.57]; raw group sign-flip $p=0.7927$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.7927$). Neither controller contrast is significant before or after Holm correction. The controlled but secondary Direct-minus-Arm-outcome contrast is 0.39 percentage points (group-bootstrap 95\% interval [\ensuremath{-1.82}, 2.64]); it also does not establish superiority. The development-selected safe policy changes success by 1.95 percentage points relative to continuation. These tests answer the frozen comparisons; a favorable unadjusted subset is not substituted for them.

Best fixed replanning scores 55.34\% versus 54.43\% for Direct. The secondary Direct-minus-Best-fixed contrast is \ensuremath{-0.91} percentage points (group-bootstrap 95\% interval [\ensuremath{-3.09}, 1.31]). Best fixed records 74 recoveries and 55 disruptions, compared with 26 and 14 for Direct: 48 additional recoveries accompany 41 additional disruptions, leaving 7 additional successful draw-level contrasts. These descriptive counts do not identify why the fixed rule scores higher.

Direct selects continuation on 245/355 eligible prefixes, warning on 4, replanning on 106, and rollback on 0. Matched selects rollback on 71 eligible prefixes. Direct records 26 draw-level recoveries and 14 disruptions. Matched intervenes on 78.0\% of prefixes, with 66 recoveries and 51 disruptions. Direct uses 27.4 calls, 42560 input tokens, and 63.4 client-seconds per planned task, versus 28.1, 43557, and 64.9 for continuation. These summaries are descriptive.
